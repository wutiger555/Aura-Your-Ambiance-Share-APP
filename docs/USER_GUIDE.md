## 📱 First Time Setup

### Step 1: Welcome to Aura

When you first open Aura, you'll experience an 8-second narrative journey:

1. **Two globes appear** - representing you and your partner in different places
2. **The Aura logo emerges** - symbolizing the bridge between your worlds
3. **Energy lines connect** - showing the power of technology to unite
4. **The heartline forms** - previewing your living connection

*Just watch and let the story unfold. It sets the emotional tone for your experience.*

---

### Step 2: Create Your Couple Profile

You'll be asked to personalize your connection:

#### Names & Emojis
- **Your name** (e.g., "Tzu-Hui")
- **Partner's name** (e.g., "Alex")
- **Your emoji** (optional, e.g., 🌸)
- **Partner's emoji** (optional, e.g., 🌙)

#### Relationship Milestones (Optional)
- **When did you start dating?** - See "Together for X days"
- **Next meeting date?** - Countdown to reunion
- **When did you last meet?** - Days since you were together

*Don't worry! You can skip these and add them later.*

---

### Step 3: Set Your Location

1. **Enter your city name**
   - Type any major city (e.g., "Taipei", "New York", "London")
   - See the cyan marker pulse on your location

2. **Enter your partner's city**
   - Same as above
   - Pink marker shows their location

*Tip: Use major cities for best results. The app uses OpenStreetMap for geocoding.*

---

### Step 4: Watch Your Worlds Connect

A beautiful 10-second animation plays:

1. **Globes breathe together** - synchronized heartbeat
2. **World map appears** - showing actual geographic locations
3. **Cities are named** - "Your City ✦ Their City"
4. **Heartline draws between you** - the living connection forms
5. **Transition to main screen** - your shared sky emerges

### Key Elements

#### 🌐 Location Globes (Top & Bottom)

Each globe shows:
- **Name with emoji** (e.g., "🌸 Tzu-Hui")

- **Temperature** (in °C)
- **Current local time**
- **Status message** (tap ✏️ to edit)
- **Sunrise/sunset times**

*Tap the edit icon to update your status message!*

#### 🎨 Blended Sky (Background)

Your partner's sky and your sky merge into one:
- **Top half** = Partner's atmosphere
- **Bottom half** = Your atmosphere
- **Middle** = Beautiful gradient blend

**Dynamic Weather Effects:**
- ☀️ **Sunny**: Gentle particle drift
- ☁️ **Cloudy**: Soft drifting clouds
- 🌧️ **Rainy**: Realistic raindrops
- ❄️ **Snowy**: Gentle snowfall
- ⚡ **Thunderstorm**: Lightning flashes

#### 💫 Living Heartline (Center)

The curved line connecting you features:
- **Breathing animation** - Pulses every 4 seconds
- **Bidirectional particles** - Energy flows both ways
- **Distance display** - How far apart you are
- **Time difference** - Hours between your time zones
- **DST warnings** - Yellow alert if time change coming

*Tap the heartline to open Time Bridge!*

## ⏰ Time Bridge

**Purpose:** Visualize and compare your 24-hour daily rhythms

### How to Use

1. **Tap the heartline** or DST indicator
2. See a **dual timeline** showing:
   - Both locations' current times
   - Sunrise and sunset for each location
   - Time difference highlighted

### Features
- **24-hour format** - Midnight to midnight
- **Sun/moon indicators** - Visual day/night markers
- **Current time markers** - See "now" for both of you
- **Timezone abbreviations** - EST, JST, GMT, etc.

*Close by tapping outside the modal or the X button*

## ⚙️ Settings & Connection Info

**Access:** Tap the gear icon (⚙️) at the top

### What You'll Find

#### 🗺️ World Map View
- Flight path animation between your cities
- Accurate geographic visualization
- Distance and estimated flight time

#### ✈️ Connection Stats
- **Distance**: Kilometers/miles apart
- **Flight time**: Estimated travel duration
- **Airports**: Nearest major airports
- **CO₂ estimate**: Environmental impact of flight

#### 💚 Best Time to Call
Shows the optimal window for video calls based on:
- Your daily schedules (if set)
- Time zone difference
- Typical waking hours

#### ✏️ Edit Features
- **Location nicknames**: Personalize city names
- **Daily rhythm**: Set your schedules
- **Reset connection**: Start fresh setup

## 📅 Daily Rhythm Editor

**Access:** Settings → "Edit Daily Rhythm"

### Purpose
Help find the best times to call/chat by comparing your daily schedules.

### How to Set It Up

#### 1. View Mode: Side-by-Side
You'll see two columns:
- **Left**: Your schedule
- **Right**: Partner's schedule

#### 2. Edit Times
Tap any time field to customize:
- **Sleep hours** (e.g., 23:00 - 07:00)
- **Work/school** (e.g., 09:00 - 17:00)
- **Busy periods** (meetings, classes, etc.)

#### 3. Quick Templates
Choose a preset schedule:
- 🎓 **Student**: School hours + study time
- 💼 **Office Worker**: 9-to-5 schedule
- 🌙 **Night Owl**: Late sleep, late wake
- 🌅 **Early Bird**: Early rise, early sleep
- 🔄 **Flexible**: Minimal fixed hours

#### 4. Visual Timeline
See three rows:
1. **Your activities** (cyan markers)
2. **Partner's activities** (pink markers)
3. **Overlapping free time** (green highlights)

### 💚 Best Times for Calls

After setting schedules, you'll see:
- **Specific time windows** when both are free
- **Duration** of each overlap
- **Your local time → Their local time** for clarity

**Example:**
```
💚 14:00-16:00 (Your Time) → 03:00-05:00 (Their Time)
   Duration: 2 hours
```

## 💝 Personalization Features (v2.5.0)

### Status Messages

**Update your current mood/situation:**
1. Tap the ✏️ icon on your globe
2. Type your message (e.g., "Working late tonight", "Miss you ❤️")
3. Save
4. Your partner sees it instantly (on next app open)

**Ideas for status messages:**
- "Just woke up ☕"
- "Busy with exams 📚"
- "Free to call tonight! 💚"
- "Missing you extra today 💕"

### Relationship Milestones

**Edit your dates:**
1. Go to Settings
2. Scroll to "Relationship Milestones"
3. Tap "Edit"
4. Update:
   - Relationship start date
   - Next meeting date
   - Last met date

**What you'll see:**
- "Together for 487 days" (auto-calculated)
- "Only 23 days until you reunite!" (countdown)
- "It's been 45 days since you last met" (if set)

### Message Center

**Leave sweet notes for your partner:**
1. Tap the message icon
2. Write your message
3. Add an emoji decoration (optional)
4. Save

*Messages persist until deleted - like leaving love notes!*

### Weather Reminders

Automatic contextual cards appear when:
- Your partner might need an umbrella (rain forecast)
- Temperature difference is significant
- Extreme weather conditions

**Example:**
```
☔ Your partner might need an umbrella today!
It's raining in Tokyo.
```

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Animations Not Playing?
```bash
# Clear cache and restart
npm run mobile -- --clear
```

### Location Not Found?
- Try using the full city name
- Use major cities for best results
- Check spelling

### Time Seems Wrong?
- Make sure device time is correct
- App uses IANA timezones from weather API
- DST changes handled automatically

### Weather Not Updating?
- Pull down to refresh
- Check internet connection
- Open-Meteo API updates every 15 minutes

## 💡 Understanding the Design

### The "Night to Dawn" Philosophy

Every element in Aura follows this narrative:

- **Night** (separation) → **Dawn** (connection)
- **Blue/Purple** (loneliness) → **Pink/Yellow** (warmth)
- **Static** (data) → **Living** (breathing, flowing)

### Color Meanings

- **🔵 Cyan/Blue**: Represents you (User)
- **🔴 Pink**: Represents your partner
- **🟡 Yellow**: Sunrise, warmth, connection
- **🟣 Purple**: Twilight, transition
- **🟢 Green**: Shared time, overlap, compatibility
- **🟠 Yellow Warning**: Time changes (DST)

### Animation Philosophy

All animations serve emotional purposes:
- **Breathing** = Shared breath across distance
- **Particles** = Energy exchange
- **Pulsing** = Living heartbeat
- **Gradients** = Merging worlds

## 📖 Glossary

- **Blended Sky**: The merged visual atmosphere combining both locations' weather and time
- **Heartline**: The curved, breathing line connecting your locations
- **Living Connection**: Animations that make the connection feel alive (breathing, particles)
- **Aura Globe**: The circular display showing location, weather, and status
- **Time Bridge**: Modal comparing 24-hour timelines
- **Daily Rhythm**: Your customizable daily schedule
- **DST**: Daylight Saving Time - time zone adjustments
- **IANA Timezone**: International standard for timezone identification
